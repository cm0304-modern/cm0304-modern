# CM 03/04 Modern 25.0.0

A fan-made, unofficial update that brings Championship Manager 03/04 (4.1.5) into the 2025/26 football world. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the original developers or publishers. No game files are included other than our installer patch and data.

## Highlights

- Players and staff from Football Manager 26 data: about 148,000 players and about 47,000 staff, with current club names, stadiums and finances.
- 28 nations with real 2025/26 divisions and cups, tested through full seasons and season rollovers: England, Spain, Italy, France, Germany, Scotland, Holland, Portugal, Switzerland, Turkey, Czech Republic, Sweden, Wales, Northern Ireland, Argentina, Brazil, Norway, Croatia, Republic of Ireland, Slovakia, Austria, Denmark, Poland, Hungary, Greece, Finland, Ukraine, Belgium.
- Modern squad rules (12 named, 5 substitutes used), 2025/26 foreign-player limits where the game can express them, EU flags, national-team managers, African Cup of Nations and year fixes.
- A Contributors screen in the game.
- 14 nations are not selectable (Serbia and Montenegro, Australia, Japan, Russia, Mexico, Colombia, Indonesia, South Korea, Hong Kong, South Africa, India, China PR, Singapore, Israel); their clubs remain as foreign clubs.

## Install

You need CM 03/04 version 4.1.5 on Windows 10 or 11, with the game closed.

1. Download `CM0304-Modern-25.0.0-Setup.exe` below.
2. Run it and accept the administrator prompt (Windows may warn about an unknown publisher: the installer is not code-signed).
3. Choose the folder containing `cm0304.exe` and let setup finish.
4. Start a **new game**. Old saves do not work.

The installer backs up the originals to `backup_pre_v25` in your game folder. To uninstall, run `Uninstall CM 03-04 25.0.0.exe` from the game folder.

## Known issues

- 14 nations are not selectable (see above).
- Some clubs have generated squads and some clubs have no manager.
- Reserve/B teams cannot be represented; youth teams are empty.
- Club and competition histories are still from 2003.
