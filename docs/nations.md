## Not selectable (14 nations)

These nations are hidden on the new-game screen. Their clubs still exist in the world as foreign clubs with current rosters, so players can move to and from them.

- Serbia and Montenegro
- Australia
- Japan
- Russia
- Mexico
- Colombia
- Indonesia
- South Korea
- Hong Kong
- South Africa
- India
- China PR
- Singapore
- Israel

## Everything else

Nations not listed above keep the game's original club structure and competitions. They are not part of the tested list.
