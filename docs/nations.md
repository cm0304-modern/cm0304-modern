# Nations

## Full 2026/27 treatment (30 nations)

These nations have real 2026/27 divisions and cups, current clubs, names, stadiums and finances, built from Football Manager 27 data. The first 28 nations are the full-treatment nations of the 25.x line; Russia and Israel are new in 26.0.0.

Domestic cups and play-offs follow the 2026/27 structure as far as the old game engine allows. Some limits are listed in [known issues](known-issues.md). In 26.0.0 the divisions of Turkey (second division 20 clubs), Wales (Cymru Premier 16), Northern Ireland (Championship 16), Russia (FNL 18) and Israel (Premier League 14, Liga Leumit 16) have the sizes of the real 2026/27 season.

## Not selectable (12 nations)

These nations are hidden on the new-game screen. Their clubs still exist in the world as foreign clubs, with rosters from the older Football Manager 26 data, so players can move to and from them.

- Serbia and Montenegro
- Australia
- Japan
- Mexico
- Colombia
- Indonesia
- South Korea
- Hong Kong
- South Africa
- India
- China PR
- Singapore

## The 25.x line

In 25.x (the 2025/26 game) 28 nations have the full treatment and 14 nations are hidden: the 12 above plus Russia and Israel. Turkey's second division has 18 clubs there, the Cymru Premier 12 and the Northern Irish Championship 12.

## Everything else

Nations not listed above keep the game's original club structure and competitions. They are not part of the tested list.
